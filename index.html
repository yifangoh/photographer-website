Hello, I’m Alice.

I photograph weddings and portraits, mostly for people who feel awkward in front of a camera. I arrive early, stay quiet, and let the day happen around me. Most of what ends up in your gallery is the in-between: the laugh just before the vows, your dad fixing your sister’s collar.

A wedding is usually eight to ten hours of coverage. A portrait session runs about an hour. You’ll have your gallery within four weeks. I take a small number of bookings each season so every one gets proper attention.
